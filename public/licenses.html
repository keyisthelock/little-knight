Privacy & product status

Your imported games, preferences and practice history stay in this browser's storage or the Windows app's LocalProfile folder. There are no analytics, ad trackers, cloud chat calls or live game integrations. Downloading the website still makes ordinary requests to its host. Backups contain your saved data; keep them somewhere you trust.

Optional email/password accounts store your email, a salted password hash, hashed session tokens and temporary anti-abuse counters in Cloudflare D1. Passwords are sent only over HTTPS and are not stored in browser storage. A Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite session cookie expires after 24 hours. Games and coaching history remain local, separated by account; they do not sync between devices and are not encrypted against someone with access to this browser. Account beta does not yet provide email verification, password recovery or self-service account deletion. Guest mode remains available.

The rating guess is a recreational, uncalibrated evaluation-loss heuristic. Dialogue is authored and event-driven, not an unrestricted language model. Prices shown in Preferences are planned prices. There is no payment processing in this preview.
